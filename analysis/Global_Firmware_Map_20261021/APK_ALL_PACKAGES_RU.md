## Important dependencies

- Excluding `zxstore` cannot remove system `PackageInstaller`.
- FLauncher must be a working HOME before stock `zxlauncher` exclusion; keep an ADB recovery fallback.
- `KuSettings` is a UI migration/removal target. `KuSetupWizard` is separate provisioning; do not remove as apparent duplicate.
- `RJBluetoothSpeaker` is a possible A2DP sink and not necessarily related to Bluetooth headphone output.
- `preinstall_del` packages require checking first-boot installation policy and pre-existing `/data` installs.
- APEX, platform permission, TV input, framework, Bluetooth and SystemUI are not cleanup candidates based solely on names.
- Runtime and AndroidManifest privilege effects still require live tests on BOTH revisions.
